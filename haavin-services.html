Stop 00 · About Haavin

A partner,
not just a
contractor.

Haavin Services was established in 2010 to deliver structured waste management for property managers, commercial buildings, industrial sites and project work across the Klang Valley. Our work is built around three things: consistent scheduling, clean collection points and accountable delivery on every site. A high-rise block and a stadium event are different problems, but both get matched to the right people, the right collection method and a schedule that fits how the site actually runs.

01

Consistent scheduling

Fixed routes and frequencies that hold up over long contracts. Sites know exactly when the truck arrives.

02

Clean collection points

Discipline at bins and holding zones so shared spaces stay clear, compliant and free of overflow.

03

Accountable delivery

A named supervisor per account and an open escalation channel for overflow or peak nights.

Stop 04 · How we work

A five-step engagement.

It mirrors how live sites actually run, from the first walkthrough to a service review that rolls changes into the next cycle.

01

Site assessment

Walkthrough of access, volume and operating hours. We note hygiene-sensitive points and overflow risks before quoting.

02

System recommendation

Match the site to the right hauling system, frequency and crew composition. Quote and contract terms confirmed.

03

Implementation

Bin placement, holding-point setup, schedule briefing with site contacts, and a supervisor assigned to the account.

04

Collection, hauling, disposal

Scheduled execution under SWCorp-licensed handling. The escalation channel stays open for overflow or peak nights.

05

Service review

Periodic review with the client on schedule fit, incidents and changes. Adjustments roll into the next cycle.
